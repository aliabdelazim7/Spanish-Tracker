# Educational Content & Pedagogical Guidelines

## 1. Educational Philosophy
Ruta Español is designed specifically for Arabic speakers learning Spanish, with special emphasis on Egyptian cultural and linguistic cognitive bridges.

### 1.1 Spanish Variant (Target: Castilian / España)
- Target dialect is **Castilian Spanish (Castellano de España)**.
- Phonetics note: Distinguish the interdental "c/z" sound (pronounced like the Arabic ث: *gracias* / *cereza*).
- Plural informal pronoun: *vosotros* / *vosotras* is taught alongside *ustedes*.

### 1.2 Pedagogical Bridges (Egyptian Arabic Annotations)
- **Ser vs. Estar**:
  - *Ser* corresponds to fundamental identity, origin, and permanent qualities ("أصله وطبيعته الثابتة: هو مصري").
  - *Estar* corresponds to temporary physical states or geographical location ("حالته المؤقتة أو مكانه دلوقتي: موجود في مدريد، أو تعبان").
- **Verbo Gustar**:
  - Emphasize that *Gustar* functions like "بيعجبني كذا" rather than the English "I like".

## 2. CEFR Level Structure

```
A1: Beginner (الأساسيات والتعارف)
A2: Elementary (المواقف اليومية والماضي)
B1: Intermediate (إبداء الرأي وصيغة الشك)
B2: Upper Intermediate (الطلاقة والمناقشات المعقدة)
C1: Advanced (المرونة المهنية والأساليب البلاغية)
C2: Mastery (الإتقان الفائق والاستيعاب التلقائي)
```

> **Pedagogical Note**: Completing level C2 signifies high functional second-language mastery according to CEFR standards; it does not claim to make a learner an automated native heritage speaker.

## 3. Seeded Starter Curriculum

### A1 - Unit 1: Saludos, Presentaciones y Cortesía (التحيات والتعارف واللباقة)
- **Lesson 1**: ¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas?
  - Introduces: *llamarse*, greetings (*Hola, Buenos días*), basic introductions (*Mucho gusto*).
  - Exercises: Multiple choice, translation, fill in the blank, word ordering, matching pairs.
- **Lesson 2**: ¿De dónde eres?
  - Introduces: *verbo Ser*, nationalities (*egipcio, española*), question structures (*¿De dónde eres?*).
- **Lesson 3**: Pedir en la cafetería
  - Introduces: *Por favor*, *café con leche*, *la cuenta*, polite requests.

### Interactive Stories
1. **Llegada a Madrid (A1)**: Arriving at Barajas airport, buying a Multi card, and navigating the metro to Puerta del Sol.
2. **Un café en Malasaña (A2)**: Ordering traditional breakfast (tostada con tomate y aceite) in Madrid's bohemian district.
